_[[The Anchor's Guild]] vs [[The Tidal Church]]_
## **The Conflict**

Nocthollow is beginning to change.

At first, it was small:

- Canal water rising slightly above normal levels
- Reflections rippling even when the surface is still
- Citizens reporting dreams of **distant waves**

Now…

The water doesn’t recede anymore.

---
## **The Tidal Church’s Plan**

The Tidal Church has begun a hidden operation beneath Nocthollow:

> **They are trying to turn the city into a second Threnovar.**

Deep beneath the canals, in forgotten flooded tunnels, they are:

- Performing **drowning rituals**
- Summoning something from below
- Slowly replacing fresh water with **“living tide”**

Their goal:

> To awaken a **Drowned God fragment** beneath the city  
> and let Nocthollow “ascend” through submersion

To them, this is not destruction.

It is **salvation**.

---
## **The Anchor Guild’s Response**

The Anchor Guild has detected the shift.

They have issued a silent directive:

> **“Stabilize Nocthollow. Prevent Full Submergence.”**

Which translates to:

- Hunt down Tidal agents in the city
- Seal underwater ritual sites
- Eliminate corrupted civilians if necessary
- Contain the entity beneath the canals

But there’s a problem.

---
## **The Complication**

Nocthollow is already unstable.

The Arcana reacts to pressure.

As the Tidal Church pushes from below…

Reality itself begins to fracture:

- Reflections start acting independently
- Some Anchors hear **voices from the water**
- Certain Arcana powers become… unpredictable

Meaning:

> The more the Anchors try to “fix” the city—  
> the more unstable it becomes.

---
## **Ideological Clash**

This isn’t just a battle.

It’s a belief war.

### ⚓ Anchor Guild:

- Reality must remain stable
- Power must be controlled
- Change is dangerous

### 🌊 Tidal Church:

- Change is natural
- Stability is stagnation
- The world must evolve—even if it drowns

---
## **Key Event: The Black Tide Rising**

At some point in your campaign:

- The canals overflow
- Parts of the city begin to **sink**
- A massive ritual begins beneath the main district

Both factions move at once.

Players must choose:

- **Stop the ritual** → preserve the city, but something beneath may remain
- **Let it happen (or fail to stop it)** → Nocthollow becomes partially submerged, forever changed.

---
## **What This Means for Players**

You are not just fighting enemies.

You are deciding:

> Should the world remain stable…  
> or be allowed to change into something unknown?

And the worst part?

Both sides… might be right.
