[[Barnabas Crimson]] and [[Tessia Von Regalia]] 

The two met outside of The Rose & Thistle Apothecary, uncanny situations but they made a connection. Then Tessia had a problem, he offers help, as a doctor and playboy he can't endure the frown of the young maiden. 

As the talks deepens Barnabas discovered that she is a Attendant of a Noble, and not just any noble. She is working with the most influencial noble in Nocthollow, [[Lucille Brightheart]] and its house the Brighthouse Family. 

Barnabas shocked to see how tall is the residence of the noble. inside he see the whole vicinity of Nocthollow, from the greenland to vast ocean. 

Lucille ask him if he can diagnose what is happening to him, with a price of course. So Barnabas doesn't hesitate. As he undergone the diagnose, Barnabas feels that it is not like any other sickness, it is not even a sickness, so he took another route, he check if he is sick with magic. And as he do that, Barnabas see a vision of a man hammered in a cross and hanged upside-down, he feels a sudden chills inside his body and completely stunned for a second like he see a divine being. He called the final verdict, Lucille is Cursed.
