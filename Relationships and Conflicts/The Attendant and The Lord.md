[[Lucille Brightheart]] & [[Tessia Von Regalia]]

---
## **The Surface Relationship**

To outsiders, it is simple:

- **Lucille Brightheart** — The Lord of House Brightheart
- **Tessia Von Regalia** — His loyal attendant

She stands behind him.  
She speaks when permitted.  
She obeys without hesitation.

> A perfect noble-attendant relationship.

---
## **The Reality**

That perception is… incomplete.

Their bond is not built on status.

> It is built on **trust and necessity**.

---
## **Lucille’s View of Tessia**

Lucille does not see Tessia as a mere servant.

He sees:

- A **constant presence** in a world of shifting loyalties
- A **stabilizing force** against unseen threats
- Someone who perceives dangers he cannot

He allows her something rare:

> **Honesty.**

She can question him.  
Correct him.  
Warn him.

And he listens.

---
## **Tessia’s View of Lucille**

Tessia does not serve out of obligation.

She chooses to stay.

She sees:

- A man carrying the weight of an entire system
- Someone who must remain composed, even when wrong
- A Lord surrounded by power—but isolated by it

Her loyalty is not blind.

> It is **intentional**.

---
## **Power Balance**

On paper:

- Lucille holds absolute authority
- Tessia is beneath him

In truth:

- Lucille controls the **world outside**
- Tessia controls what reaches him from **within**

If Lucille is the mind of House Brightheart—

> Tessia is its **instinct**.

---
## **Unspoken Rules Between Them**

They never needed to say these aloud:

- Tessia does not interfere with Lucille’s decisions
- Lucille does not ignore Tessia’s warnings
- Neither lies to the other

This is why their partnership works.

---
## **Moments That Define Them**

- Tessia quietly stopping a threat before Lucille notices
- Lucille continuing a conversation, fully aware Tessia is handling danger behind him
- A single glance between them replacing entire conversations

---
## **Emotional Undercurrent**

Their relationship is not openly emotional.

But it is not empty.

- There is **respect**
- There is **understanding**
- There is a quiet form of **dependence**

Not weakness.

> Precision.

---
## **How Others See It (If They’re Perceptive)**

Most see:

> A noble and his attendant.

A few realize:

> She is not just standing beside him
> she is part of why he stands at all.

---
## **If One Is Threatened**

- If Lucille is targeted → Tessia becomes relentless
- If Tessia is threatened → Lucille responds decisively and without hesitation

Neither overreacts.

But neither forgives.

---
## **What They Represent Together**

Separately:

- Lucille = **Control of Systems**
- Tessia = **Control of Life & Instinct**

Together:

> **Perfect Stability**

A system that thinks… and survives.

---
## **Dual Signature Lines**

**Lucille:**

> “Stay close. There are variables I do not account for.”

**Tessia:**

> “That is why I am here.”
