_The Empress of [[The World of Veilrune]] (Third Epoch)_  
_Bearer of the Throne of Dominion_

---
### **Basic Information**

- **Name:** Rosellia Gustav
- **Title:** Empress of Veilrune
- **Alias:** “The Sovereign of Silence”
- **Race:** Human (Ascended)
- **Era:** Third Epoch
- **Affiliation:** Imperial Throne of Veilrune
- **Status:** Dead???
---
### **Appearance**

Rosellia embodies authority in its purest form.

- Golden-blonde hair braided into a crown-like weave
- Piercing amber-gold eyes that seem to weigh everything they see
- Pale, flawless skin—almost statue-like
- Wears regal armor fused with ceremonial robes, etched in ancient runes
- A mantle of white and gold drapes over her shoulders like a symbol of absolute rule

Her presence is overwhelming.

Not loud. Not aggressive.

> Simply undeniable.

---
### **The Third Epoch**

Rosellia ruled during a time when:

- Arcana power was unstable and spreading
- City-states clashed for dominance
- Early cults (including proto-Tidal factions) began emerging

Under her rule:

> Veilrune did not collapse.  
> It unified.

---
### **Her Reign**

Rosellia did what no ruler before her could:

- United the **12 Major and Minor Cities** under one imperial doctrine
- Established early forms of **Arcana regulation** with Secret Organization.
- Suppressed uncontrolled awakenings or corruptions.
- Built the foundation of what would later become groups like the Anchor Guild

But—

It came at a cost.

---
### **The Throne of Dominion**

Rosellia was not just a ruler.

She was bound to an **Arcana Authority**:

> **The Throne**

An entity, power, or concept that granted:

- Absolute command over those who acknowledged her authority
- The ability to “stabilize” reality within her domain
- Influence over fate, obedience, and structure

But the Throne demanded one thing:

> Control. Always control.

---
### **Personality**

Rosellia was:

- Calm and absolute
- Highly intelligent and far-seeing
- Emotionally distant—but not unfeeling

She believed:

> “Freedom without order is destruction.”

She did not enjoy ruling.

She accepted it as necessity.

---
### **Controversy of Her Rule**

To some, she was:

- A savior
- A unifier
- The reason Veilrune survived

To others, she was:

- A tyrant
- A suppressor of Arcana evolution
- The one who chained the world to “stability”
---
### **The Disappearance**

At the end of the Third Epoch:

Rosellia vanished.

No body.  
No successor with equal authority.

Only fragments of records remain:

- The Throne was “sealed”
- Something beneath Veilrune awakened
- Or… she ascended beyond human existence
---
### **Legacy in the Present**

Even now:

- The Anchor Guild echoes her ideology
- Noble systems mirror her structure
- Arcana regulation traces back to her laws

Some believe:

> She is not gone.

Only waiting.

---
### **Player Hooks**

- Discover relics tied to her Throne
- Uncover truth behind her disappearance
- Encounter remnants of her authority
- Decide whether her rule should return
---
### **Optional Arcana (Legendary Tier)**

**Arcana:** The Emperor (Absolute Authority)

**Concept: Dominion**

- Commands cannot be easily resisted
- Reality stabilizes in her presence
- Chaos weakens under her rule

At full power:

> Disobedience itself becomes difficult.

---
### **What She Represents**

If modern factions fight for control—

> Rosellia was control.

Not influence.  
Not power.

**Authority made absolute.**

---
### **Signature Line**

> “A world without order does not deserve to stand.”
