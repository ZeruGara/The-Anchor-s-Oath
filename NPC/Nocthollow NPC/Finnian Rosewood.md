- **Name:** Finnian Rosewood

---
### **Appearance**

Finnian carries a soft, approachable presence that contrasts Edward’s intensity.

- Light brown/blonde tousled hair
- Gentle golden-green eyes behind round glasses
- Warm, calm expression
- Wears layered apothecary attire with yellow accents
- Carries multiple vials, notes, and a well-used formula book

He always looks like he’s mid-thought—

> Or mid-discovery.

---
### **Public Persona**

Finnian is what most people expect from an apothecary:

- Kind
- Patient
- Attentive

He handles:

- Healing remedies
- Safe potions
- Consultations and diagnoses

If Edward is who you go to for _risk_—

> Finnian is who you trust with your life.

---
### **True Nature**

Finnian is not naive.

He understands:

- Exactly how dangerous alchemy can be
- Exactly what Edward is capable of

And still chooses to stand beside him.

> “Healing isn’t the opposite of harm… it’s understanding it.”

---
### **Personality**

Finnian is:

- Gentle but not weak
- Highly intelligent
- Deeply empathetic

He listens more than he speaks.

But when he does—

> He’s usually right.

---
### **Relationship with Edward**

They are not opposites.

They are **complements**.

- Finnian stabilizes Edward
- Edward sharpens Finnian
- Finnian prevents excess
- Edward pushes boundaries

They trust each other completely.

> “He walks the edge. I make sure he doesn’t fall.”

---
### **Hidden Depth**

Finnian has a side few see:

- Can create compounds just as dangerous as Edward
- Understands poisons well enough to weaponize cures
- Capable of making life-saving or life-ending decisions instantly

He simply chooses restraint.

---
# **Apothecary Features (Narrative)**

Finnian specializes in:

- Healing mixtures
- Buffing elixirs
- Stabilization compounds
- Emergency battlefield support

His creations are:

> Reliable. Clean. Efficient.

# **Traits**

### **Master Apothecary**

Finnian can create and apply potions as a **bonus action**.

---
### **Refined Formulas**

All healing done by Finnian:

- Gains +5 bonus HP
- Removes either poison, disease, or one debuff effect
---
### **Stabilizing Presence**

Allies within 10 ft:

- Gain advantage on death saving throws
- Cannot be instantly killed unless reduced below -20 HP
---
# **Actions**

### **Restorative Injection**

_Melee or ranged (20 ft)_

- Ally regains **4d8 + 5 HP**
- Ends one condition (poisoned, frightened, restrained)
---
### **Purifying Mist (Recharge 5–6)**

20-ft radius cloud:

- Allies heal **3d8 HP per turn (2 turns)**
- Enemies have disadvantage on attack rolls
---
### **Catalyst Flask**

Thrown within 30 ft:

Choose effect:

- **Boost:** Allies gain +2 to all rolls (1 turn)
- **Suppress:** Enemies -2 to all rolls (1 turn)
---

# **Bonus Actions**


### **Quick Remedy (3/day)**

- Target instantly regains **20 HP**
---
### **Emergency Stabilize**

- A creature at 0 HP instead goes to **1 HP**
---
# **Reactions**

### **Perfect Counteragent**

When an ally is affected by poison or condition:

- Negate the effect completely
---
# **Tactics**

Finnian avoids direct combat.

He:

- Stays behind allies
- Constantly heals and buffs
- Controls tempo of battle
- Keeps key allies alive at all costs

If paired with Edward:

> They become extremely hard to defeat.

---
### **Signature Line**

> “Careful… not everything I make is meant to hurt.”
