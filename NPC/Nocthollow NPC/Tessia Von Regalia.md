_Attendant of House Brightheart_  
_The Silent Verdant Flame_

---
### **Basic Information**

- **Name:** Tessia Von Regalia
- **Race:** Half Human / Wood Elf
- **Role (Public):** Personal Attendant of House Brightheart of [[Nocthollow – The Moon]]
- **True Role:** Arcane Advisor & Hidden Protector
- **Class:** Sorcerer (Innate Magic — Verdant/Bloodline)
- **Affiliation:** House Brightheart
- **Status:** Active

---
### **Appearance**

Tessia carries a quiet, natural elegance—less refined than high elves, but more grounded.

- Long silver hair with a soft braid along one side
- Emerald-green eyes, sharp yet distant
- Slightly pointed ears marking her elven heritage
- Wears a pristine white uniform with gold embroidery
- Always gloved, always composed

She often appears… deep in thought.

As if listening to something unseen.

---
### **Public Persona**

To outsiders, Tessia is simply:

- A loyal attendant
- A composed and respectful servant
- The one who manages Lucille Brightheart’s immediate affairs

She speaks softly, acts precisely, and never oversteps.

> The perfect attendant.

---
### **True Role in House Brightheart**

Tessia is far more than a servant.

> She is the **arcane safeguard** of the Brightheart lineage.

Her duties include:

- Monitoring Arcana fluctuations around Lucille
- Detecting magical threats before they manifest
- Advising on supernatural risks
- Silently eliminating unseen dangers

Most don’t even realize she’s a sorcerer.

That’s intentional.

---
### **Personality**

Tessia is:

- Calm and introspective
- Observant to a near unsettling degree
- Emotionally restrained, but not cold

She rarely interrupts.

But when she speaks—

> It matters.

---
### **Sorcerous Origin — Verdant Bloodline (LoTM Flavor)**

Tessia’s power is tied to something ancient and natural.

**Theme:** Growth, life, and quiet inevitability

Her abilities manifest as:

- Subtle control over organic matter
- Sensing life and emotional disturbances
- Magic that grows stronger over time rather than explosively

But there’s a darker side:

> Growth… can become overgrowth.

---
### **Abilities (Narrative)**

- Can sense living beings within a wide radius
- Detects hidden or disguised entities
- Manipulates vines, roots, or natural constructs in combat
- Slowly drains energy from prolonged proximity (very subtle)

Her magic is not flashy.

It is **inevitable**.

---
### **Relationship with Lucille Brightheart**

Their relationship is unique:

- Tessia is absolutely loyal
- Lucille trusts her completely
- She is one of the _very few_ who can speak honestly to him

But—

Her loyalty is not blind.

It is **chosen**.

> “My duty is to you. Not your power.”

---
### **Presence**

Tessia is easy to overlook.

Until you try to do something dangerous.

Then—

You realize she was watching the entire time.

---
### **Player Hooks**

- She evaluates the players before allowing access to Lucille
- Acts as a quiet ally or observer
- Warns players of unseen magical threats
- Might test them without them realizing

---
### **Subtle Suspicion**

Players may notice:

- Plants react slightly when she passes
- She seems to know who is about to act aggressively
- She appears behind people… without sound

---
### **What She Represents**

If Lucille is control of systems—

> Tessia is control of **life itself, quietly maintained**.

Not domination.

Not force.

**Balance.**

---
### **Signature Line**

> “Life reveals more than words ever will.”

# **Traits**

### **Verdant Awareness**

Tessia automatically senses living creatures within **30 ft**, even if hidden or invisible (unless magically concealed).

---
### **Innate Sorcery**

Tessia has advantage on saving throws to maintain concentration.

---
### **Verdant Aura**

Creatures of Tessia’s choice within 10 ft:

- Regain **5 HP at the start of their turn**

Enemies in the aura:

- Have disadvantage on saving throws vs her spells

# **Actions**

### **Verdant Binding (Recharge 5–6)**

Tessia targets a point within 60 ft:

- Vines erupt in a 20-ft radius
- Creatures must succeed **DC 17 Strength save** or be **restrained**
- Area becomes difficult terrain
---
### **Life Drain Touch**

_Melee Spell Attack:_ +9 to hit  
**Hit:** 18 (4d8) necrotic damage

Tessia heals half the damage dealt.

---
# **Bonus Actions**

### **Bloom Step (3/Day)**

Tessia teleports up to 30 ft, leaving behind faint blooming vines.
### **Sustain Life**

Choose one ally within 30 ft:

- Gains **15 temporary HP**
---
# **Reactions**

### **Verdant Shield**

When hit, Tessia gains:

- +5 AC (like _Shield_)
- Attacker takes **2d8 poison damage**
---
# **Tactics**

Tessia does not fight aggressively.

She:

- Controls the battlefield (_Plant Growth, Binding_)
- Protects key allies (especially Lucille)
- Disables threats rather than killing
- Uses _Greater Invisibility_ to reposition

If forced—

> She becomes extremely difficult to approach.

---
# **Behavior**

- Watches silently before acting
- Targets the most dangerous individual first
- Rarely wastes spells

---
# **With Lucille Brightheart**

If Lucille is within 30 ft:

- Tessia gains **advantage on all saving throws**
- She can use reactions to **redirect attacks to herself**

> She will always protect him first.

---
# **Combat Feel**

You don’t overpower Tessia.

> You get slowed, restrained, drained… and outlasted.

---
### **Signature Combat Line**

> “Struggling only hastens what is already growing.”
