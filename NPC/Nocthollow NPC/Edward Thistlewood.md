_The Gilded Poisoner_

---
### **Basic Information**

- **Name:** Edward Thistlewood
- **Alias:** “The Gilded Hand”
- **Role:** Apothecary / Alchemical Specialist
- **Affiliation:** Rose & Thistle Apothecary
- **Spouse:** Finnian Rosewood
- **Location:** [[Nocthollow – The Moon]] — Brass market
- **Status:** Active
---
### **Appearance**

Edward stands out immediately—less like a merchant, more like someone who _knows exactly how dangerous he is_.

- Pale skin, sharp features
- Messy silver-white hair
- Striking golden eyes with an unsettling calm
- Wears a dark, tailored coat with gold accents and arcane insignias
- Accessories: rings, chains, and a pendant vial (always filled with something unknown)

His posture is relaxed… almost careless.

> But nothing about him is careless.

---
### **Public Persona**

To customers, Edward is:

- Charismatic
- Smooth-talking
- Slightly provocative

He handles:

- “Special requests”
- Rare concoctions
- Clients who don’t ask too many questions

If Finnian is the heart of the shop—

> Edward is the edge.

---
### **The Apothecary — _Rose & Thistle_**

The shop itself reflects both owners:

- **Thistle (Edward):** healing, restoration, balance
- **Rose (Finnian):** toxins, stimulants, unstable mixtures

Edward’s side includes:

- Poisons (legal… and otherwise)
- Experimental elixirs
- Performance enhancers with side effects
- Anti-Arcana suppressants

Everything is labeled.

Not everything is safe.

---
### **True Nature**

Edward is not just an apothecary.

He is:

- A chemist of dangerous precision
- A manipulator of effects, not just substances
- Someone who understands how small doses change everything

> “Everything is poison. It’s just a matter of timing.”

---
### **Personality**

Edward is:

- Confident, almost playful
- Intellectually sharp
- Morally flexible—but not reckless

He enjoys:

- Testing limits
- Subtle experimentation
- Watching how people react

But he is not chaotic.

> He always knows where the line is.

And how close he can get to it.

---
### **Relationship with Finnian Rosewood**

Their dynamic is contrast—and balance.

- Finnian heals
- Edward harms
- Finnian stabilizes
- Edward experiments

But:

- Edward respects Finnian deeply
- Finnian keeps Edward grounded

They trust each other completely.

> “He makes the cure. I make sure it’s needed.”

---
### **Reputation in Nocthollow**

- Known for **high-quality but risky products**
- Sought out by:
    - Adventurers
    - Assassins
    - Anchor Guild operatives (quietly)

People say:

> If Edward made it… it works.

Whether you survive it is another question.

---
### **Hidden Depth (Optional)**

Edward may have:

- Tested substances on himself
- Built resistance to toxins
- Knowledge of rare Arcana-altering compounds

There are rumors:

> Some of his creations shouldn’t exist.

---
### **Player Hooks**

- Purchase rare or dangerous potions
- Commission custom brews
- Get caught in one of Edward’s “tests”
- Be offered something… too useful to refuse

---
### **Interaction Style**

Edward enjoys conversation—but it’s always layered.

> “Tell me what you need… and what you’re willing to risk for it.”

---
### **What He Represents**

If Finnian is **life and restoration**—

> Edward is **risk, consequence, and controlled danger**.

---

# **Traits**

### 🌿 **Acolyte of Balance**

Edward treats **poison damage as divine expression of nature**:

- His poison damage **ignores resistance**
- Creatures immune to poison instead take **half damage**

---
### **Toxic Physiology**

Due to constant exposure:

- Advantage on saves vs poison & disease
- Immune to the poisoned condition
---
### **Nature’s Duality**

Whenever Edward casts a spell that restores HP, he can choose:

- Heal normally **OR**
- Convert it into **necrotic damage** to an enemy within 30 ft (same amount)

# **Channel Divinity (2/rest)**

### **Turn the Rot**

As an action:

- All enemies within 30 ft take **4d10 poison damage**
- Must succeed **Con save DC 17** or be **poisoned for 1 minute**
### **Bloom or Wither**

Choose one effect in a 20-ft radius:

- **Bloom:** Allies regain **30 HP**
- **Wither:** Enemies take **30 necrotic damage**
---
# **Actions**

### **Thistlebrand Dagger**

_Melee Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit  
**Hit:** 6 (1d4 + 2) piercing + 14 (4d6) poison

Target must succeed **DC 17 Con save** or:

- Take additional **3d6 poison damage**
- Disadvantage on attack rolls for 1 turn
---
### **Toxic Mist (Recharge 5–6)**

20-ft radius cloud within 60 ft:

- Heavily obscured
- Creatures take **4d8 poison damage** on entry/start
- Area counts as difficult terrain
---
# **Bonus Actions**

### **Distilled Injection (3/day)**

Edward applies a compound:

- **Ally:** Gains advantage on all rolls for 1 turn
- **Enemy:** Disadvantage on all rolls for 1 turn
---
# **Reactions**

### **Nature’s Reversal**

When a creature within 30 ft heals:

- Edward can redirect half that healing as **damage** to another creature
---
# **Tactics**

Edward fights like a strategist, not a brawler:

- Opens with **control (Spike Growth / Spirit Guardians)**
- Applies **poison pressure over time**
- Uses healing offensively via **Nature’s Duality**
- Targets high-threat enemies with debuffs

If paired with Finnian:

> He becomes significantly more dangerous.

---
# **Combat Feel**

Edward doesn’t overwhelm you instantly.

> He weakens you… layer by layer… until you realize you’re already losing.
