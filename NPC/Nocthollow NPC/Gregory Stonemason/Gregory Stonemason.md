_Owner of Lone Steel Blacksmith_  
_The Man Who Shapes What Lasts_

___
### **Basic Information**

- **Name:** Gregory Stonemason
- **Alias:** “Old Stone”
- **Role:** Master Blacksmith
- **Affiliation:** Independent
- **Location:** [[Nocthollow – The Moon]]— Brass Market District
- **Status:** Active

---
### **Appearance**

Gregory looks like he was carved out of the same material he works with.

- Older man, late 50s to early 60s
- Broad, heavy build with thick arms
- Short gray beard, always dusted with ash
- Deep-set eyes, calm but unyielding
- Wears a thick leather apron, scarred from years of work

His hands are rough, scarred, and steady.

They don’t shake.

---
### **His Shop — _Lone Steel Blacksmith_**

Unlike the noisy chaos of other forges, Gregory’s shop is… quiet.

- A single forge, always burning steadily
- Tools arranged with near-perfect order
- Weapons and armor displayed simply—no decoration, just quality

The sign reads:

> **LONE STEEL**

Because Gregory works alone.  
And always has.

---
### **What He Makes**

Gregory doesn’t mass-produce.

He crafts:

- Reliable weapons built to endure real combat
- Armor designed for survival, not appearance
- Custom pieces tailored to the user

His work is known for one thing:

> It does not fail when it matters.

---
### **Public Persona**

Gregory is:

- Quiet
- Direct
- Unimpressed by status or titles

He doesn’t flatter customers.

If you bring him poor material or bad ideas:

> He’ll tell you.

---
### **Personality**

Gregory believes in one principle:

> “If it breaks, it was never good enough.”

He values:

- Patience
- Discipline
- Simplicity

He dislikes:

- Flashy weapons
- Overengineered designs
- Anything that sacrifices function for style
--- 
### **Reputation in Nocthollow**

- Considered the most reliable blacksmith in the district
- Trusted by veterans and long-time Anchors
- Avoided by those looking for quick or cheap work

Some say:

> If Gregory agrees to make something for you…  
> you’re worth his time.

---
### **Subtle Depth (Optional)**

There are rumors:

- Gregory once forged weapons for something far worse than the Guild
- He stopped taking certain commissions years ago
- He recognizes materials others don’t

And sometimes…

He refuses jobs without explanation.

---
### **Player Hooks**

- Commission high-quality or custom weapons/armor
- Repair damaged gear that others can’t fix
- Be tested before he agrees to work for you
- Learn that what you ask for… says something about you
---
### **Interaction Style**

Gregory doesn’t talk much.

But when he does, it matters.

> “Tell me what you need. Not what you want.”

--- 
### **What He Represents**

Gregory is not power.  
Not mystery.  
Not chaos.

> He is **reliability**.

In a world that bends and breaks—

He makes things that don’t.

---
### **Signature Line**

> “Good steel doesn’t care what you’re fighting. It just holds.”
