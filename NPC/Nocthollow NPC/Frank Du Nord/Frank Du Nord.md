_The Black Hand of [[Nocthollow – The Moon]]_  
_Leader of the Midnight Market_

---
### **Basic Information**

- **Name:** Frank du Nord
- **Alias:** “The Broker of Shadows”
- **Role:** Leader of Nocthollow’s Black Market
- **Affiliation:** Independent (but connected to all factions)
- **Status:** Active
- **Influence:** City-wide, especially in canals and lower districts

---
### **Appearance**

Frank is not what people expect from a crime lord.

- Mid-40s, well-groomed, always composed
- Slicked-back dark hair with streaks of gray
- Wears elegant but understated suits—never flashy
- Always carries a cane (not for support… or so it seems)
- His eyes are calm, observant… and always calculating

He smells faintly of tobacco and sea salt.

---
### **Public Persona**

Officially?

Frank du Nord does not exist.

Unofficially, he is known as:

- A merchant of rare goods
- A middleman for “difficult acquisitions”
- Someone who can get _anything_… for the right price

People don’t meet Frank by choice.

> They are introduced… when they’re already in too deep.

---
### **True Role in Nocthollow**

Frank controls the **Midnight Market**, a hidden network dealing in:

- Illegal Arcana artifacts
- Information and secrets
- Blackmail material
- Smuggled enhancements and forbidden items

If something is too dangerous for the surface…

> It ends up in Frank’s hands.

---
### **Power and Influence**

Frank doesn’t rule with violence.

He rules with **information**.

- He knows who is corrupted
- Who owes what
- Who is lying—and why

Even the Anchor Guild avoids unnecessary conflict with him.

Because Frank knows things…

> He shouldn’t know.

---
### **Personality**

Frank is:

- Polite, almost charming
- Patient—never rushed
- Extremely dangerous when crossed

He never raises his voice.

He doesn’t need to.

> “Business is built on trust… and leverage.”

---
### **Dealings with Other Factions**

- **Anchor Guild:** Tense neutrality
    - He occasionally provides information… at a cost
- **Clockwork Lodge:** Active trade
    - Supplies rare materials and test subjects
- **Tidal Church:** Quiet hostility
    - He refuses to deal with them directly

> “Some things don’t bargain. They just take.”

---
### **Hidden Truth (Optional)**

Rumors say Frank is not just a man.

- He has survived situations no normal human should
- He appears unchanged over many years
- Some believe he is bound to an **unknown Arcana**

Or worse—

> He doesn’t use power. He **trades in it**.

---
### **Player Hooks**

Players may:

- Seek him for rare Arcana items
- Be forced into a deal they regret
- Discover he already knows about them
- Realize they’ve been part of his plan from the start

---
### **What He Represents**

Frank is not chaos.

He is **control through information**.

In a city of secrets—

> He owns the truth.

---
### **Signature Line**

> “Everything has a price. You just haven’t realized yours yet.”
