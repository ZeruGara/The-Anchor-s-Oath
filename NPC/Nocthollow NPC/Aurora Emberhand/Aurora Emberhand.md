_Owner of “Heavy Metal”_  
_Supplier of What Keeps You Alive_

---
### **Basic Information**

- **Name:** Aurora Emberhand
- **Alias:** “Aurora of Heavy Metal”
- **Role:** Shop Owner (Provisions & Adventuring Essentials)
- **Affiliation:** Independent
- **Location:** [[Nocthollow – The Moon]] — Brass Market District
- **Status:** Active

---
### **Appearance**

Aurora looks like someone who’s seen what bad preparation does to people.

- Medium height, sturdy build
- Auburn hair tied back in a practical knot
- Light soot stains on her sleeves (from packing, not forging)
- Wears layered utility clothing with many pockets
- Often has gloves tucked into her belt

Her eyes are sharp—always sizing up what you _actually_ need.

---
### **Her Shop — _Heavy Metal_**

Despite the name, _Heavy Metal_ isn’t a forge.

It’s one of the most reliable **supply shops in Nocthollow**.

The sign is a worn iron plate stamped with bold letters:

> **HEAVY METAL**

Inside, you’ll find:

- Rations and water supplies
- Rope, lanterns, oil, and tools
- Travel packs and survival kits
- Basic weapons and armor maintenance gear
- Medical supplies and emergency kits

Everything is organized, labeled, and practical.

No clutter. No nonsense.

---
### **Why the Name?**

When asked why it’s called _Heavy Metal_, Aurora usually shrugs:

> “Because what you carry decides if you make it back.”

---
### **Public Persona**

Aurora is known as:

- Straightforward
- Reliable
- Blunt—but fair

She doesn’t try to impress customers.

She makes sure they **don’t die unprepared**.

---
### **Personality**

Aurora is:

- Practical over everything
- Quietly protective of regular customers
- Intolerant of reckless adventurers

If you try to buy something useless for your situation, she’ll say it.

> “You don’t need that. You need this.”

---
### **Reputation in Nocthollow**

- Trusted by locals and travelers alike
- Known to give the _right_ gear, not the most expensive
- Has likely equipped more survivors than any other shop

Even Anchors stop by.

---
### **Subtle Depth**

Aurora notices things:

- Who’s injured but hiding it
- Who’s scared
- Who isn’t coming back

She doesn’t ask questions.

But sometimes she’ll quietly add something extra to your pack.

---
### **Player Hooks**

- Buy essential gear before missions
- Get optimized loadouts based on your quest
- Receive warnings about dangerous areas
- Be refused service if acting careless

She might say:

> “Come back when you’re serious about staying alive.”

---
### **What She Represents**

Aurora isn’t about power or mystery.

> She is preparation.

In a world of Arcana, cults, and unstable reality...

She makes sure you at least have a chance.

---
### **Signature Line**

> “You don’t need more gear. You need the right gear.”
