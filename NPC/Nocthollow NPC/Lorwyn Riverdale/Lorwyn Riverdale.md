_The Silver Broker of the South Side_  
_Rival to Frank du Nord_

---
### **Basic Information**

- **Name:** Lorwyn Riverdale
- **Alias:** “The Silver Dealer”
- **Race:** Half-Elf (High Elf lineage)
- **Role:** Owner of the South Side Black Market
- **Affiliation:** Independent
- **Location:** [[Nocthollow – The Moon]] — South Market District
- **Status:** Active

---
### **Appearance**

Lorwyn looks refined in a way that feels almost out of place in Nocthollow.

- Tall, slender, with graceful posture
- Pale skin with faint elven features
- Long silver-blond hair, usually tied neatly
- Sharp, calculating eyes—cold but intelligent
- Wears elegant coats with subtle arcane embroidery

Everything about him is **intentional**.

Nothing is accidental.

---
### **His Domain — South Side Market**

Unlike Frank’s hidden network, Lorwyn’s operation is:

- Structured
- Layered
- Controlled like a system

His market deals in:

- Refined Arcana artifacts
- Rare magical components
- High-end illegal goods
- Information… but selectively

It’s quieter than Frank’s network.

More exclusive.

> And far more dangerous to cross.

---
### **Public Persona**

Lorwyn presents himself as:

- Polite
- Cultured
- Professional

He speaks like a noble, not a criminal.

And that’s exactly the point.

> He doesn’t act like he runs a black market—  
> he acts like he **owns a business empire**.

---
### **True Nature**

Lorwyn is not just a merchant.

He is a **strategist**.

- Every deal is calculated
- Every alliance is temporary
- Every rival is studied

Including Frank.

Especially Frank.

---
### **Personality**

Lorwyn is:

- Calm and composed
- Highly intelligent
- Subtly arrogant

He believes:

> “Control is not taken. It is designed.”

He doesn’t rely on chaos.

He builds systems that **outlast it**.

---
### **Hidden Strength (Optional)**

Lorwyn is rumored to possess:

- Advanced arcane knowledge beyond normal traders
- Access to rare High Elf techniques
- A personal collection of dangerous artifacts

Some say he doesn’t just sell power.

> He **curates it**.

---
### **Player Hooks**

- Gain access to rare, high-tier items
- Be pulled into the rivalry between him and Frank
- Become pawns in a larger strategic play
- Realize Lorwyn planned your involvement
---
### **How He Treats Players**

Lorwyn doesn’t intimidate.

He evaluates.

> “You are either an asset… or a variable.”

And he decides which very quickly.

---
### **What He Represents**

If Frank is **information control**—

> Lorwyn is **perfect execution through planning**.

Not chaos.  
Not brute force.

Just inevitability… designed in advance.

---
### **Signature Line**

> “I don’t deal in chances. Only outcomes.”
