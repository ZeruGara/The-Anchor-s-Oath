# The-Anchor-s-Oath
In the world of Veilrune, the age of iron and smoke has risen over older things. Brass engines thunder beneath cobbled streets, towering clockworks pierce the skyline, and gaslight burns against the endless dusk. Yet beyond the reach of industry, ancient cathedrals still cast long shadows, forgotten graves still whisper, and fate still marks the chosen in ways no machine can explain. 

Fate starts to chose its anchor to defeat the enders of this world, will you take the oath as it's protector? Or you will choose your own destiny?
