_“Flesh fails. Steel endures.”_

---
Far from the fog-choked canals of Nocthollow lies [[Vesperfall – The Tower]].
a city of brass towers, hissing pipes, and endless ticking.

Here, the night is not silent.

It hums.

At the center of this industrial labyrinth stands a place both revered and feared:

**The Clockwork Lodge.**

---
### **What They Are**

The Clockwork Lodge is a guild of **inventors, engineers, and body-modifiers** who believe one truth above all:

> **Human limits are flaws meant to be corrected.**

Where others fear the Arcana…

the Lodge seeks to **replicate, enhance, and surpass it through machinery**.

---
### **Their Work**

Within their iron halls, they create:

- Mechanical prosthetics that outperform natural limbs
- Clockwork organs that replace failing bodies
- Devices that mimic Arcana abilities
- Experimental augmentations that blur the line between human and construct

Some call it innovation.

Others call it **madness**.

---
### **Why Vesperfall?**

Vesperfall thrives on progress—no matter the cost.

- Smoke fills the skies
- Factories never sleep
- Workers replace parts of themselves just to keep up

In this city, enhancement is not questioned.

It is expected.

---
### **What They Hide**

The Lodge does not simply improve the body.

They are trying to **engineer control over the unknown**.

Rumors whisper that:

- Some members no longer feel human
- Failed experiments are “recycled”
- Certain constructs still _think_… long after their creators are gone

And worst of all—

They may be getting closer to something that rivals the Arcana itself.

---
### **What This Means for You**

If you are an Anchor…

then the Clockwork Lodge represents something dangerous:

Not chaos.

Not corruption.

But **controlled evolution**.

And that may be far harder to stop.
