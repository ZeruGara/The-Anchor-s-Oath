“Small lights. Endless dark.”

The **Firefly Rebellion** is a decentralized resistance movement born in the depths of **Blackbarrow**.

They are not an army.

They are not even a single organization.

They are a **network of sparks**—workers, smugglers, medics, couriers, and defectors—united by one belief:

> _“If the world above refuses to see us, we will make it impossible to ignore us.”_

## **Origins**

Blackbarrow was built from what the upper cities discarded:

- Broken workers
- Failed systems
- Forgotten lives

For years, survival was enough.

Until the shortages worsened.

Until entire districts were sealed off “for safety.”

Until people started disappearing—not dying, just… **removed from record**.

The first Firefly wasn’t a leader.

Just someone who lit a lantern in a collapsed tunnel and wrote:

> _“We are still here.”_

Others followed.

Lights appeared across Blackbarrow.

And a rebellion began.

## **Philosophy**

The Fireflies don’t believe they can overthrow the Major Cities.

Not directly.

Instead, they focus on:

- **Disruption over domination**
- **Exposure over destruction**
- **Survival over victory**

They fight in ways that:

- slow systems
- reveal corruption
- protect their own

> _“A single flame dies quickly. A hundred flickers cannot be stamped out.”_

## **Structure**

The Fireflies are intentionally fragmented.

### **Cells (“Lanterns”)**

- Small groups (3–10 members)
- Operate independently
- Often don’t know other cells

### **Guides**

- Experienced members who coordinate between cells
- Rarely stay in one place
- Often use aliases

### **The Glow**

- Not a person—but a concept
- Refers to the **shared mission**
- Orders are passed as suggestions, not commands

This makes the faction:

- Hard to destroy
- Hard to control
- Sometimes chaotic

## **Operations**

### **Sabotage**

- Disrupt supply lines to upper cities
- Break machinery that exploits Blackbarrow labor

### **Information Leaks**

- Steal records from Valecourt or Giltmere
- Expose hidden laws, debts, and disappearances
