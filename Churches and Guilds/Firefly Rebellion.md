“Small lights. Endless dark.”

The **Firefly Rebellion** is a decentralized resistance movement born in the depths of **Blackbarrow**.

They are not an army.

They are not even a single organization.

They are a **network of sparks**—workers, smugglers, medics, couriers, and defectors—united by one belief:

> _“If the world above refuses to see us, we will make it impossible to ignore us.”_

## **Origins**

Blackbarrow was built from what the upper cities discarded:

- Broken workers
- Failed systems
- Forgotten lives

For years, survival was enough.

Until the shortages worsened.

Until entire districts were sealed off “for safety.”

Until people started disappearing—not dying, just… **removed from record**.

The first Firefly wasn’t a leader.

Just someone who lit a lantern in a collapsed tunnel and wrote:

> _“We are still here.”_

Others followed.

Lights appeared across Blackbarrow.

And a rebellion began.

## **Philosophy**

The Fireflies don’t believe they can overthrow the Major Cities.

Not directly.

Instead, they focus on:

- **Disruption over domination**
- **Exposure over destruction**
- **Survival over victory**

They fight in ways that:

- slow systems
- reveal corruption
- protect their own

> _“A single flame dies quickly. A hundred flickers cannot be stamped out.”_

## **Structure**

The Fireflies are intentionally fragmented.

### **Cells (“Lanterns”)**

- Small groups (3–10 members)
- Operate independently
- Often don’t know other cells

### **Guides**

- Experienced members who coordinate between cells
- Rarely stay in one place
- Often use aliases

### **The Glow**

- Not a person—but a concept
- Refers to the **shared mission**
- Orders are passed as suggestions, not commands

This makes the faction:

- Hard to destroy
- Hard to control
- Sometimes chaotic

## **Operations**

### **Sabotage**

- Disrupt supply lines to upper cities
- Break machinery that exploits Blackbarrow labor

### **Information Leaks**

- Steal records from Valecourt or Giltmere
- Expose hidden laws, debts, and disappearances

### **Smuggling**

- Move people, goods, and messages through hidden tunnels
- Often coordinate with **Rookhaven couriers**

### **Relief Work**

- Provide medicine, food, and shelter
- Protect vulnerable communities

## **Methods (Important Tone)**

The Fireflies avoid unnecessary harm.

But they are not harmless.

They will:

- sabotage infrastructure
- manipulate systems
- endanger powerful targets

And when pushed—

They are willing to escalate.

## **Notable Figures**

### **Commander Ilyra Venn**

- Calm, precise, rarely seen
- Former strategist (possibly from Grimwick or Valecourt)
- Believes in controlled rebellion—not chaos

> _“We are not here to burn the world. We are here to prove it was already burning.”_

### **Lysa (Valecourt Contact)**

- Embedded in bureaucracy
- Feeds the Fireflies information
- Specializes in altering records and legal gaps

### **“Cinder” Ral**

- Cindergate defector
- Expert in explosives and machinery
- Pushes for more aggressive action

### **Mother Sable**

- Runs underground clinics
- Protects civilians above all else
- Opposes violent escalation

## **Symbol**

A **small flame with wings**—often drawn in chalk, ink, or carved into metal.

- Appears in hidden places
- Marks safe routes
- Signals recent activity

## **Reputation**

### In Blackbarrow:

- Seen as protectors
- Or reckless troublemakers

### In Major Cities:

- Classified as **insurgents**
- Blamed for disruptions and leaks

### In Valecourt:

- Officially: _nonexistent_
- Unofficially: _a growing concern_
