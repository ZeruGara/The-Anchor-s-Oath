---
### **Appearance**

Leon is not flashy—but he is unforgettable once noticed.

- Tall, broad-shouldered, always standing straight
- Short dark blonde hair, slightly unkempt
- Sharp, steady eyes that rarely blink under pressure
- Wears a long, reinforced coat with subtle anchor engravings inside
- Always carries at least one firearm, usually holstered but never far

His presence is… quiet.

Not intimidating at first—  
but something about him feels **final**.

---

### **Public Persona**

To outsiders, Leon is:

- A silent church guard
- A personal escort for “dangerous visitors”
- Someone who handles problems discreetly

He speaks little, often only:

> “This way.”  
> “Stay behind me.”

People don’t question him.

They instinctively comply.

---
### **True Role in the Guild**

Leon is a **Guild Agent**, but more specifically:

> **He is the Anchor Guild’s executioner.**

When a case is beyond saving…  
when an Arcana bearer cannot be contained…

Leon is sent.

---
### **His Function**

Leon is deployed when:

- A target must be eliminated immediately
- A mission becomes too dangerous to prolong
- The Guild has already made its decision

He does not investigate.

He does not negotiate.

> He ends things.

---
### **Personality**

Leon is calm, direct, and unwavering.

- Speaks only when necessary
- Acts without hesitation
- Shows no outward emotion in combat

But he is not mindless.

He simply believes:

> “Hesitation is how things get worse.”

---
### **Relationship — [[Laura Brumhilde]]**

Laura decides.

Leon acts.

- He trusts her judgment completely
- Rarely questions her conclusions
- Moves the moment she signals

To others, it’s unsettling.

To them, it’s normal.

> She sees the truth.  
> He makes it real.

### **Indomitable (2/Day)**

Reroll a failed saving throw.

---
# **Gunslinger Features**

### **Firearm Expert**

Leon ignores the **loading property** and does not suffer disadvantage in close range.

---
### **Deadeye Shot (3/Turn)**

Before hitting, Leon may choose one effect:

- **Precise Shot:** +2 to hit
- **Brutal Shot:** +PB damage
- **Disarming Shot:** Target drops held item (Str save DC 17)
- **Piercing Shot:** Hit passes through target to another behind

---
### **Quickdraw**

Leon has **advantage on initiative rolls**, and on the first turn:

- His attacks deal **+2d6 damage**

---
# **Arcana Feature — Judgment (Reckoning)**

_(Laura decides. Leon enforces.)_

### **Marked for Execution**

As a bonus action, Leon marks one creature within 60 ft for 1 minute:

- Deals **+1d8 damage per hit**
- If the target has harmed an ally, damage becomes **+2d8**

Uses = 3/day

---

### **No Escape**

Marked targets:

- Cannot benefit from invisibility against Leon
- Have disadvantage on saving throws vs his effects

---
### **Final Shot (1/Day)**

When Leon hits a marked target below half HP:

- The attack deals **+6d10 damage**
- Target must succeed a **DC 17 Con save** or be reduced to **0 HP**
