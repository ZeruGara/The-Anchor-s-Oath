_Bounty Hunter of the [[The Anchor's Guild]] 
Tracker of the Unstable_

---
### **Basic Information**

- **Name:** Elema Grimwell
- **Alias:** “The Hound of Nocthollow”
- **Role (Public):** Church Retrieval Agent
- **True Role:** Bounty Hunter — Arcana Tracker
- **Affiliation:** Anchor Guild
- **Status:** Active
- **Specialty:** Locating and capturing rogue Arcana bearers

---
### **Appearance**

Elema looks like someone who doesn’t stay in one place for long.

- Lean, athletic build
- Shoulder-length dark hair, often tied loosely
- Wears a hooded coat layered with belts, tools, and hidden pockets, also seen with her pet crow.
- Carries multiple weapons—none decorative
- Her eyes are sharp, constantly scanning, rarely resting

There’s always dirt on her boots.

And something… tense in her posture.

Like she’s listening to something no one else can hear.

---
### **Public Persona**

To outsiders, Elema is:

- A retrieval specialist for the Church
- Someone who brings back “lost” individuals
- A quiet, no-nonsense operative

She doesn’t reassure people like Laura.

She doesn’t disappear like Leon.

She asks questions.

And she already knows when you’re lying.

---
### **True Role in the Guild**

Elema is the one sent **before things become irreversible**.

> If Leon is the end…  
> Elema is the hunt.

She tracks:

- Newly awakened Arcana users
- Rogue Anchors
- Targets who fled Guild control
- Entities trying to stay hidden

Her job is simple:

> Find them first.

---
### **Her Function**

Elema is deployed when:

- A target escapes containment
- Someone shows early signs of instability
- The Guild needs someone _alive_

She prefers capturing over killing.

But she will not hesitate if things turn.

---
### **Personality**

Elema is:

- Direct and blunt
- Observant to an uncomfortable degree
- Restless when not working

She doesn’t like waiting.

She trusts patterns more than people.

> “Everyone leaves a trail. You just have to see it.”

---
### **Arcana (Optional Reveal)**

**Arcana:** The Moon (Reversed — Fear)

- She senses emotional disturbances and intent
- Can track targets through lingering fear or stress
- Becomes harder to detect the closer she gets to her prey

This makes her terrifying to fugitives—

Because hiding doesn’t stop fear.

---
### **Relationships**

- **Laura Brumhilde:** Respects her, but finds her unsettling
- **Leon Brumhilde:** Works with him when needed—prefers not to
    
    > “If he’s here, I took too long.”

---
### **Reputation Among Anchors**

Known as:

- “The Hound”
- “The Tracker”
- “The One You Don’t See Until It’s Too Late”

Some recruits fear her more than Leon.

Because with Leon…

It’s already over.

With Elema…

> You still have time to realize you’re being hunted.

---
### **Player Hooks**

- She is sent to track the players (early tension)
- She becomes an uneasy ally on a mission
- She tests whether the players are “stable”
- She may decide… they’re not

---
### **What She Represents**

Elema is not judgment.  
She is not execution.

> She is inevitability in motion.

The moment someone tries to run—

She starts walking.

---
### **Signature Line**

> “You can keep moving… I’ll still catch up.”

# **Traits**

### **Hunter’s Focus**

Elema has advantage on **Survival checks** to track creatures and always knows the direction of a creature she has damaged within the last 24 hours.

---
### **War Magic**

When Elema uses her action to cast a cantrip, she can make one weapon attack as a bonus action.

---
### **Bound Weapon**

Elema is bonded to her weapon. She cannot be disarmed unless incapacitated and can summon it to her hand as a bonus action.

---
### **Relentless Pursuer**

Opportunity attacks against creatures moving away from Elema are made with **advantage**.

---
# **Spellcasting (Eldritch Knight)**

**Spellcasting Ability:** Intelligence (DC 14, +6 to hit)

Elema is a 10th-level spellcaster.

---

- _Minor Illusion_

# ⚔️ **Actions**

### **Multiattack**

Elema makes **three melee attacks**.

---
### **Arcane Longsword**

_Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft  
**Hit:** 9 (1d8 + 5) slashing damage

If under _Booming Blade_:

- Target takes extra **2d8 thunder damage** if it moves
---
### **Throwing Blade**

_Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, range 20/60 ft  
**Hit:** 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage

---
### **Arcane Snare (Recharge 5–6)**

Elema targets a creature within 30 ft:

- Must succeed **DC 15 Strength save** or be **restrained** by spectral bindings
- Target can repeat save at end of turns

---
# **Bonus Actions**

### **Hunter’s Step (3/Day)**

Elema teleports up to 30 ft to a space she can see.

---
### **Mark the Prey**

One creature within 60 ft is marked for 1 minute:

- Elema deals **+1d6 damage** to it
- Target cannot benefit from hiding from her

---
# **Reactions**

### **Shield (Spell)**

+5 AC until start of next turn.

---
### **Pursuer’s Strike**

When a creature within reach attempts to leave:

- Elema makes a melee attack
- On hit, target’s speed becomes **0 for that turn**
---
# **Tactics**

Elema fights like a hunter, not a soldier:

- Marks priority target immediately
- Uses **Hold Person / Arcane Snare** to immobilize
- Applies pressure with _Booming Blade_
- Uses _Misty Step_ to stay close
- Prevents escape at all costs

---
# **Behavior**

- Focuses one target relentlessly
- Ignores distractions unless necessary
- Prefers capture—but will kill if needed

---
# **With the Guild**

- Works best **alone or in small teams**
- If Leon is present → she defers the kill
- If Laura is present → she trusts the target selection

---
### **Signature Combat Feel**

You don’t fight Elema.

> You try to get away from her.
