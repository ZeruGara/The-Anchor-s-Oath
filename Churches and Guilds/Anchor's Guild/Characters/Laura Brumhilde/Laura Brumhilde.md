_Receptionist of the Anchor’s Church (Public)_  
_Gatekeeper of the [[The Anchor's Guild]] (Truth)_

---
### **Basic Information**

- **Name:** Laura Brumhilde
- **Role:** Front Desk Receptionist, Anchor’s Church (Nocthollow)
- **True Role:** Intake Officer & First Evaluator of Arcana Cases
- **Affiliation:** Anchor Guild
- **Status:** Active
- **Notable Relation:** Wife of **[[Leon Brumhilde]]**

---
### **Appearance**

Laura is always seen behind the reception desk—calm, composed, and impossible to read.

- Blonde shoer hair
- Pale complexion, almost porcelain under candlelight
- Wears simple business casual attire with a silver **anchor pendant** and long elegant black hand gloves
- Eyes that seem… slightly unfocused, as if watching something else

Her smile is polite.

Always the same.

---
### **Public Persona**

To the citizens of Nocthollow, Laura is:

- Kind
- Patient
- Soft-spoken

She greets everyone warmly, listens carefully, and never raises her voice.

If someone is distressed, she reassures them:

> “You’re safe here. The Anchor holds.”

No one suspects her.

----
### **True Role in the Guild**

Laura is the **first filter** of the Anchor Guild.

Before any mission begins…  
before any investigation is approved…

> **Everything passes through her.**

She decides:

- Who is telling the truth
- Who is hiding something
- Who is already too far gone

And sometimes…

Who doesn’t leave the building.

---
### **Personality (True)**

Laura is not cold.

But she is **precise**.

- She values stability above emotion
- She speaks gently—even when making harsh decisions
- She rarely shows fear

She believes:

> “If we hesitate… the world breaks.”

---
### **Arcana (Optional Reveal)**

**Arcana:** The High Priestess (Upright – Secrets)

- She can sense hidden truths
- Detect lies, illusions, and concealed intent
- Occasionally “knows” things she shouldn’t

This is why she never needs to raise her voice.

She already knows the answer.

---
### **Relationship — [[Leon Brumhilde]]**

Leon is one of the Guild’s field Agent.

Their relationship is:

- Quiet
- Deeply trusting
- Slightly unsettling

They rarely show affection publicly.

But there’s an unspoken understanding:

> Laura decides _who_ is a threat.  
> Leon decides _what to do about it._

Some in the Guild whisper:

- Leon is her blade
- Laura is the one who points it

### **How to Use Her in Game**

- **Quest Giver:** Assigns missions calmly, always vague but accurate
- **Moral Pressure:** Approves or denies risky actions
- **Hidden Power:** Not a frontline fighter—but extremely dangerous if crossed
- **Foreshadowing Tool:** Can hint at future events without explaining

---
### **Signature Line**

> “You don’t need to explain… I already understand.”

# **Core Features**

### **Sneak Attack (1/Turn)**

+6d6 damage when she has advantage or an ally is within 5 ft of the target.

---
### **Cunning Action**

Bonus action: Dash, Disengage, or Hide.

---
### **Uncanny Dodge**

Reaction: halve damage from an attack she can see.

---
### **Evasion**

On Dex saves:

- Success → no damage
- Fail → half damage

---
### **Master of Tactics**

Bonus action: Help an ally within 30 ft.

---
# **Arcana Feature — High Priestess (Secrets)**

### **Unveiling Presence**

Laura automatically knows if a creature within 30 ft is:

- Lying
- Under illusion
- Hiding intent

(No action required)

### **Premonition (3/Day)**

After seeing a roll, Laura can:

- Add or subtract **1d8**

---
### **Silent Judgment**

Creatures Laura hits with Sneak Attack must succeed a **DC 16 Wis save** or:

- Have disadvantage on their next roll
- Cannot benefit from advantage until end of their next turn

---
# **Actions**

### **Dagger (Primary Weapon)**

+8 to hit, reach 5 ft  
Damage: 1d4 + 4 piercing + Sneak Attack

### **Hidden Needle (Ranged)**

+8 to hit, range 20/60 ft  
Damage: 1d6 + 4 piercing

On hit: target must make **DC 15 Con save** or take:

- Additional **2d6 psychic damage**
- Disadvantage on concentration

---
# **Bonus Actions**

### **Guided Strike**

Choose an ally within 30 ft:

- Gains advantage on next attack

---
# **Reactions**

**Foreseen Outcome (2/Day)**

When a creature within 30 ft rolls:

- Laura can impose **disadvantage** OR grant **advantage**

---
### **Combat Style**

Laura does **not fight like a normal rogue**:

- Stays in the backline
- Manipulates rolls and outcomes
- Supports allies while weakening enemies
- Strikes only when necessary—and always precisely

---
### **Behavior in Combat**

- Rarely speaks loudly
- Always knows who the real threat is
- Targets enemies who think they are hidden or safe

---
### **If Things Go Wrong…**

Laura will not panic.

She will calmly say:

> “You’ve already made your mistake.”

…and then act accordingly.
