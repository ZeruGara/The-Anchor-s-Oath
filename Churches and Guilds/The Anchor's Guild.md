_Publicly: The Church of the Anchor_  
_Truth: Something far more dangerous_

---

In the city of [[Nocthollow – The Moon]], nothing is ever truly clear.

Fog clings to the canals.  
Lights reflect where they shouldn’t.  
And sometimes… the reflection moves differently than you do.

Amid this uneasy stillness stands a quiet sanctuary:

The **Anchor’s Church**.

---
### **The Church Above**

To the public, it is a place of calm.

A pale stone structure rising beside black waters,  
its bells soft, its halls warm, its priests gentle.

People come to pray for:

- Stability
- Clarity of mind
- Protection from “night terrors”

And they leave… feeling just a little safer.

---
### **What Lies Beneath**

Below the church—hidden under flooded halls and sealed chambers—  
exists the true heart:

**The Anchor Guild.**

A network of operatives, watchers, and Arcana bearers who monitor Nocthollow’s distortions.

Because of all cities…

> **Nocthollow is the most unstable.**

---
### **Why Nocthollow?**

Nocthollow sits where reality is thin.

- Reflections sometimes act on their own
- Voices echo without a source
- People disappear into the fog… and sometimes return wrong

The Arcana is **stronger here**.  
And more dangerous.

So the Guild built its base where it is needed most—

> At the edge of reality itself.

---
### **The Guild’s Operations**

From beneath the Church, Anchors:

- Track Arcana awakenings in the city
- Patrol canals where distortions appear
- Seal locations where reality “slips”
- Eliminate those who can no longer be contained

All of it hidden behind sermons and prayer.

---
### **The Hidden Entrance**

Only members know the truth:

Behind the main altar, beneath a still pool of black water,  
lies a submerged passage.

To enter, one must:

- Step into the water
- Speak their Arcana’s name
- And let the reflection pull them under

If accepted…

they emerge below.

If not…

the water stays still.

---
### **What This Means for You**

To the citizens of Nocthollow, you are:

> Servants of the Anchor’s Church.

But in truth—

You walk the canals at night.  
You watch the reflections.  
You listen to the whispers.

Because in Nocthollow…

> The world is already starting to slip.

And you are the only thing holding it together.
