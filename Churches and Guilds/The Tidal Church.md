_“The sea does not reject… it reshapes.”_

---
Beyond trade routes and safe shores lies [[Threnovar - Death]] ,
a drowned region of broken coasts, sunken ruins, and black tides that never rest.

The waves there do not sound like water.

They sound like **whispers**.

And rising from those depths is a faith feared even in rumor:

**The Tidal Church.**

---
### **What They Are**

The Tidal Church is a cult-like guild of **oceanic druids and deep devotees** who worship beings known only as:

> **The Drowned Gods**

They do not see mutation as corruption.

They see it as **blessing**.

---
### **Their Belief**

To them, the surface world is flawed—rigid, fragile, temporary.

But the ocean?

It adapts.  
It consumes.  
It transforms.

So they teach:

- Flesh must change to survive
- Identity is meant to dissolve
- Perfection lies beneath the waves

> “To drown is not to die… but to become.”

---
### **What They Do**

Members of the Tidal Church:

- Perform rituals in submerged ruins
- Expose themselves and others to mutating tides
- Create hybrid beings of flesh and sea
- Spread their influence through coastal regions

Some willingly join.

Others are **taken**.

---
### **Why Threnovar?**

Threnovar is no longer fully part of the world above.

- Entire districts lie underwater
- Creatures move where no life should exist
- The tide sometimes flows **inward**, not out

It is believed the boundary between **sea and something else** is thin there.

---
### **What They Hide**

The Church claims to serve the Drowned Gods.

But no one truly knows:

- What those gods are
- If they are even gods
- Or if something is simply **answering back** from below

Some who return from Threnovar are changed.

Not just in body—

but in **mind**.

---
### **What This Means for You**

If you encounter the Tidal Church…

You are not just facing enemies.

You are facing something that:

- Doesn’t fear change
- Doesn’t fear loss
- Doesn’t even fear death

Because to them—

> **Everything ends in the ocean anyway.**
