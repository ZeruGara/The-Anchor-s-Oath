Where Aureveil had stability, Nocthollow had the opposite.

The region was plagued by:

- Thick fog
- Shifting terrain
- Strange reflections and echoes

Instead of resisting it, its people adapted.

They learned to:

- Navigate by memory instead of sight
- Communicate with the unseen
- Study the strange rather than fear it

Over time, Nocthollow became a center for:

- Séances
- experimental sciences
- and forbidden studies

It thrives not by controlling reality—

But by **accepting its uncertainty**.

It's close to [[Rookhaven (Knight of Swords)]] and [[Eldermire - The Hermit]]
