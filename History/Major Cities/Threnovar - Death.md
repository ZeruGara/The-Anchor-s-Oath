In the early days, death in Veilrune was unpredictable.

Some died and returned. Others vanished completely.

Threnovar was built by those who sought to **make death consistent**.

They created:

- structured burial systems
- legal inheritance practices
- controlled necromantic techniques

Over generations, death became:

- recorded
- regulated
- monetized

Threnovar turned mortality into a system.

And systems… bring power.
