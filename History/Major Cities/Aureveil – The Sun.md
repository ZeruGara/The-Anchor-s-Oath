
Aureveil began as a settlement that experienced the longest, most consistent daylight.

Its people learned to:

- Harness light
- Build with precision
- Measure time

This gave them an advantage over all others.

They industrialized early
creating:

- Rail systems
- Clockwork machines
- Structured governance

Aureveil became the **symbol of progress and certainty**.

But with control came expectation.

Perfection was no longer a goal it was a requirement.
