As trade between cities began, conflict followed.

Disputes, betrayals, and misunderstandings threatened to collapse what little order existed.

Valecourt emerged as the answer.

Built deliberately as a **neutral ground**, it established:

- laws
- contracts
- enforceable agreements

What began as mediation grew into domination.

Now:

- Every agreement passes through Valecourt
- Every debt is recorded
- Every action can be judged

It does not rule by force—

But by making resistance **impossible to justify**.

It's close to [[Grimwick (Swords)]] and [[Threnovar - Death]]
