Vesperfall was not always defined by ruin.

At its center once stood a massive spire built by early scholars attempting to **stabilize the Veil itself**.

They failed.

The spire was struck by a force described only as _“skyfire beyond lightning.”_

It shattered.

But instead of abandoning the place, people stayed.

They studied the aftermath.

Now, Vesperfall is a city of:

- experimentation
- invention
- calculated risk

They believe understanding failure is the only path to progress.
