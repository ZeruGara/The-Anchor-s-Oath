## **I.The Age Before Record (First & Second Epoch - Era Before Empress Rosellia Gustav)**

Before Veilrune had nations, there were no cities, only scattered peoples trying to survive a world that behaved… inconsistently.

- Days would last too long, or end too quickly.
- Reflections didn’t always match reality.
- Death was not always permanent.
- Storms appeared without warning and vanished just as suddenly.

Scholars now believe this era was when the **Veil**, the unseen layer of reality, was still unstable and bleeding into the physical world.

Humanity did not understand it.

But they learned one thing:

> Certain places were more _stable_ than others.

People gathered there.

Those places became the first foundations of the great cities.

---
## **II. The Anchoring Era(Third Epoch - Empress Rosellia Gustav's Reign)**

Over time, six regions proved uniquely resistant to the world’s instability. Each developed in isolation, shaped by the conditions that surrounded them.

These would become the **Six Major Cities of Veilrune**.

But their rise was not peaceful.

Each city had to **impose order on chaos** and in doing so, they defined what they would stand for.

## **III. The Accord of Six**

Eventually, the cities made contact by the reign of The Empress Gustav

At first, it led to tension... but also realization.

Each city had solved a different part of the same problem:

- Aureveil → control
- Nocthollow → adaptation
- Vesperfall → understanding
- Threnovar → finality
- Valecourt → order
- Eldermire → observation

Alone, each was incomplete.

Together, they formed a fragile balance.

So they established:

> **The Accord of Six**

An agreement to:

- Maintain independence
- Avoid total war
- Preserve the stability of Veilrune

Not out of trust...

But necessity.

---
## **V. The Present (Fifth Epoch) – Cracks in the System**

The balance still holds.

But it is weakening.

- Nocthollow shifts more often than before
- Vesperfall’s experiments grow increasingly dangerous
- Threnovar reports irregularities in death records
- Valecourt tightens its control, rewriting laws preemptively
- Aureveil’s perfection shows signs of strain
- Eldermire… has begun sending warnings

No one agrees on what is happening.

But all agree on one thing:

> The Veil is no longer as stable as it once was.

# **Veilrune – The Six Minor Cities**

### **“The great cities define the world. The smaller ones make it move.”**

## **I. The Age After the Accord**

After the **Accord of Six**, the world stabilized—for a time.

Trade routes opened. Knowledge spread. Systems formed.

But the Major Cities, for all their power, had limits:

- Aureveil could not supply every machine
- Valecourt could not process every transaction
- Threnovar could not manage every death
- Nocthollow could not contain every secret

Gaps formed.

And where there are gaps—

Something always rises to fill them.

## **II. The Rise of the Minor Cities**

The Minor Cities were not founded by grand design.

They emerged from:

- overflow
- neglect
- ambition
- or necessity

Each one grew in the shadow of a Major City… then became something indispensable.

### 1. [[Cindergate (Wands)]]
### 2. [[Silvershade (Cups)]]
### 3. [[Grimwick (Swords)]]
### 4. [[Giltmere (Pentacles)]]
### 5. [[Blackbarrow (Five of Pentacles)]]
### 6. [[Rookhaven (Knight of Swords)]]
---

## **III. Their Role in the Balance**

The Minor Cities do not rival the Majors.

They **support, disrupt, and connect** them.

- Cindergate fuels industry and rebellion
- Silvershade preserves memory and emotion
- Grimwick sharpens war and strategy
- Giltmere controls wealth
- Blackbarrow reveals the cost of progress
- Rookhaven moves everything between them

If the Major Cities are pillars—

The Minor Cities are the **currents beneath them**.

Unseen.

But essential.

## **IV. The Present Tension**

Recently, the Minor Cities have begun to change.

- Cindergate’s inventions grow unstable
- Silvershade reports people remembering lives they never lived
- Grimwick’s intelligence networks detect contradictions in reality
- Giltmere tracks “assets” that appear and vanish
- Blackbarrow’s population rises… without explanation
- Rookhaven delivers messages that were never sent

Individually, these are anomalies.

Together—

They suggest something deeper.

> _“The great cities hold the world together._  
> _The smaller ones feel when it starts to break.”_
