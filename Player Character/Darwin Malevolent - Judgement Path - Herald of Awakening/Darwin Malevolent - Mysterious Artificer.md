Seventeen years ago, Darwin Malevolent lived as a noble heir to a quiet but influential house—one tied to one of the most dangerous secrets in the realm. His family were not human. They were metal dragons, ancient beings who had long since abandoned open dominion in favor of concealment. 

Under human guises, they integrated into noble society, carefully masking their nature as “refined technique” rather than sorcery. 

Their true craft, however, was far more valuable—and far more dangerous. They were master producers of rare and superior metal materials, supplying entire nations with weapons, armor, and infrastructure-grade resources forged through draconic methods disguised as craftsmanship. It was not conquest that sustained them, but commerce and precision. For generations, it worked. 

Until it didn’t.
