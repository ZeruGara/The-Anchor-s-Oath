Mordain Blackshot was left alone on a drifting boat as a child, placed there by parents he never knew for reasons he never understood. The sea should have taken him but it didn’t. He was found by a feared pirate crew led by Captain Brutus, a ruthless man to the world, but a protector of lost children. Brutus took him in, and Mordain was raised aboard the ship as part of the crew. The ship became his entire life. The crew raised him like family: 

Brutus — captain and father figure 
torruk, a tortle — patient survival teacher 
Grak, an orc — combat trainer and protector 
Mira, a human woman — like an older sister and healer 
Jon, a human man — taught him firearms 
Evan, a human man — his closest childhood friend 
Lily, a goblin — sharp-eyed scout and little sister figure 
Selene, an elf — quiet, precise mentor 
Kael, an elf — rival and training partner 
Aria, a half-elf — kind presence who kept him grounded 
Mark, a human man — rough but guiding instructor 

They weren’t good people they stole, fought, and killed but to Mordain, they were family. That life ended when a noble warship attacked them, backed by a powerful spellcaster. The ship was destroyed in moments, swallowed by fire and sea. Mordain was thrown overboard and washed ashore unconscious. When he awoke, his memories were broken and scattered. He only knew fragments faces he couldn’t place, feelings he couldn’t explain and a single word that stuck in his mind: Bingo. He was later arrested after being found sleeping in an alley, completely lost and unclothed, unable to explain who he was or where he came from. Now he wanders the world as Bingo, searching for his past, his crew, and the truth of who he really is.
