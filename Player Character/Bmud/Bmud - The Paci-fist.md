_[[The Anchor's Guild]]_

Bmud was born in the back alleys of a crowded port city where survival mattered more than laws. His people were shifters, looked at with suspicion, treated like animals whenever fear took over the streets. Growing up, he learned early that fists solved problems faster than words. 

As a child, Bmud wandered between taverns, underground fighting pits, and market districts, surviving through scams, small thefts, and bare-knuckle brawls. He never stayed in one place long enough to call it home. The smell of cheap ale, sweat, and rain-soaked streets became more familiar to him than any family. Despite his rough life, Bmud carried one strange passion: music. 

Somewhere during his wandering years, an old traveling bard gave him a handmade instrument after seeing him silently listening outside a tavern window. For the first time, Bmud found something that calmed the beast inside him. He protected that instrument more fiercely than gold. But one night, after a drunken gambling fight, the instrument was stolen. Bmud never forgot it. Since then, he’s drifted from town to town as a pit fighter, mercenary, hustler, and occasional conman, chasing rumors about the thief while drowning his frustrations in alcohol and violence. 

Beneath the grime, sarcasm, and bad attitude is someone terrified of being chained down or controlled again. When fights break out, Bmud becomes terrifying, his shifter instincts awakening into savage bursts of speed and brutality. Yet outside combat, he acts lazy, rude, and uncaring, almost like he wants people to underestimate him. Truthfully, he trusts almost nobody. 

The streets taught him that attachments become weaknesses. Still… somewhere deep inside, Bmud hopes that finding his stolen instrument might also help him recover the piece of himself he lost with it. the instrument he lost.
