_[[The Anchor's Guild]]_

Framboise’s life tells a tale of two cities and a world in between. she once lived in Nocthollow, the city of mirrored alleys and forbidden laboratories. it was there she stepped through a shimmering canal and vanished into the Feywild. when she emerged, she found herself outside Aureveil, the radiant industrial capital. 

changed by her travels, she realized she could no longer blend into society. she traded with the shadows for a life on the literal edge of the city, setting up a canvas tent just outside its walls. 

she wears a high-collared, blue coat with leather stays and buckled boots. to hide her swirling, moon-colored eyes, she constantly avoids eye contact, appearing reclusive or even rude to those who try to get close. 

to the people, she is an eccentric Apothecary. she uses her Herbalism Kit to brew tinctures that she sells to factory workers, masking her druidic magic as "industrial chemistry". 



**"I don't look at the sky to see the time; I listen to the gears of Aureveil. and I don't look at you because some things—_like the moon_—aren't meant to be stared at directly. now, do you have the scrap metal for this tincture, or are we done?"**
