_[[The Anchor's Guild]]_

In a room where her convent gathers, a pool of blood is open to all acolytes. She looks across the surface of the water to see into it, to fulfill the request of one of the clergy members. At first they were skeptical that she was touched by the _gift_ and her origin clearly ignited a debate among them. She paid no attention to it, to all the scrutiny and she went on to do her duties. As her eyes go black, she did not expect that her visions would bring her backwards. Into the past whenever she wrecked havoc... The flames flicker up the sides of her hair, this silhouette completely engulfing her already red hair. Rage continues to boil in her heart, you could say that it's her nature... or is that passion? Whatever it is, she needs to channel it... to harness it. To prevent the fire from raging on and on... destruction that continues to appear beneath the tracks that she leaves. There it was, her old home.. in shambles, ashes underneath the foundation that kept it up. No one was hurt but this left a scar on them, on all of them. "I have had enough of your spawn, send her away or I'll kick you both out" his step-father uttered beneath his breath. Though her mother packed every that she might need... she still felt empty. Darkness engulfs her whole and the rustling continues in the trees, the hooting just that constant sound in the night. The cold air made that tiny flame in her hand obsolete, useless. She continues to shiver in this neck of the woods. Her shattering teeth continues to move, to overpower the owl near her. "I.. I want to go home..." The owl continues to hooting as her consciousness fade while she crumpled up into a ball. With a loud gasp she sees the marble ceiling and she'd sigh deeply so she could regain her composure. Although now she uses the night to cover up herself, to keep it shrouded from the past or from her own self. This mask continues to be her protection, there must be a reason why she was touched by the Mother's gift.
