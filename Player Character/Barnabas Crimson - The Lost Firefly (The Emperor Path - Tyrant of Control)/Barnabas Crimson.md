**BACKSTORY** 

In the soot-covered undercity beneath the iron spires of Aureveil, there once lived a boy named Evanders Ladouceur. He was beautiful in the unsettling way some statues are beautiful. Pale skin, gentle eyes, and a voice soft enough to earn the affection of priests and townsfolk alike. He served faithfully within the Church, unaware that the blood within him carried something the clergy would one day fear. His mother was a humble herbalist who treated the poor with remedies forbidden by the Church. While priests demanded gold for sanctioned medicine, she healed freely from the shadows of the lower districts. Her craft was more than herbal knowledge. It was an ancient blood-bound art passed through generations of her family. Through blood, she could understand sickness, feel the flaws within flesh, and create cures no ordinary physician could replicate. When the Church discovered the truth, they called it **witchcraft.** She pleaded before the clergy, insisting their bloodline existed to heal, not corrupt. The High Priest saw only blasphemy. She was dragged into the cathedral square and burned before the public as a warning against heresy. Evanders never saw the flames. The priests had already taken him beneath the cathedral for “purification.” That night shattered him forever. In the darkness of the chamber, something sleeping within his blood awakened. The priest who sought to destroy him suddenly found his own body collapsing from within. Veins blackened beneath his skin. Flesh weakened. Breath failed. A sickness without cure spread through him in moments. Evanders escaped through the sewers beneath the cathedral, broken and bleeding, only to find his home reduced to ash. That was the night Evanders Ladouceur died.


He was later discovered unconscious in the streets of Aureveil after being struck by the carriage of a wealthy noble family, House Crimson. Unable to bear children of their own, Lord and Lady Crimson took the boy in and raised him as their son. From then on, he became Barnabas Crimson. Aureveil transformed him. Beneath the towering cathedrals and endless factory smoke, Barnabas devoted himself to the study of flesh, medicine, and surgical science. He became obsessed with the fragility and beauty of the human body. Bone, muscle, nerve, and blood became his scripture. Years later, Doctor Barnabas Crimson became one of the most celebrated surgeons in the district. Nobles sought him endlessly for cosmetic reconstruction and delicate procedures that bordered on miraculous. His hands were said to rival divine craftsmanship itself. Kind. Elegant. Refined. That was the mask Aureveil adored. But beneath the polished smile lived a man who never forgot what the Church had done. In secret, Barnabas became involved with a rebellion organization known as the Firefly, a hidden movement working to dismantle the corruption poisoning the nation. He funded their operations, treated wounded rebels, smuggled medicine through the undercity, and used his influence among the elite to gather information. Yet even the Firefly knew little of his true experiments. Barnabas had inherited his mother’s blood. And through that blood, he began creating pathogens unlike anything the world had ever seen. Tailored afflictions engineered with surgical precision. Diseases that exposed weakness hidden beneath flesh and faith alike. To the people of Aureveil, Doctor Crimson was a miracle worker.
